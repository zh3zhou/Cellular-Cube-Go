## Recording protocol

For a significant decision record: date, status (Proposed/Accepted/Superseded/Rejected), question, chosen approach and reason, considered/rejected alternatives, source and confidence, validation and supersession links. Keep routine changes in the existing work log. Historical suggestions are Proposed unless acceptance/implementation evidence confirms them; uncertainty is explicit.

At completion of substantive work, check changes to design/interfaces/scientific assumptions/gates/durable workflow. Record an actual new decision when one occurred; otherwise note no new decision in the normal closeout. Keep frozen history and append replacements. Do not transcribe private conversations.
